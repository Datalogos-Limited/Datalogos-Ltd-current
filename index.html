01 / GOVERNANCE PLATFORM

Counterpoise

Balance innovation with control.

For teams that need policies to affect real work, Counterpoise coordinates request context, independent Governance, Privacy, Ethics and Security assessments, a deterministic decision, optional human approval and enforcement immediately before an action. Start with the domains relevant to a workflow; add more without replacing the shared control plane.

02 / DATA ASSURANCE

Verity

Know what you can trust.

For data owners, risk teams and auditors who need to explain a dataset or AI decision, Verity links source and lineage to validation rules, versions, approvals, changes and evidence. It can show what passed, what is missing and what needs review. Evidence supports assurance; it does not make a source claim true by itself.

04 / FEDERATED DATA FABRIC

Hyphvera

Many sources. Shared meaning.

For organisations that need a consistent, governed view across data held in separate platforms, Hyphvera connects source systems through metadata, catalogue and event-driven integration patterns. Source stewards retain accountability; consumers gain discoverable, contextualised data with lineage and change signals. It complements Verity: Hyphvera connects and contextualises data, while Verity assesses its assurance evidence.

ABOUT DATALOGOS

We're here for the
hard questions.

The data and AI conversation is full of promise. It is also full of decisions that cannot be left to chance.

Datalogos Limited is a UK-based challenger built around a conviction: governance should make progress possible. We bring executive advisory and practical technology into the same conversation—connecting business intent to architecture, decisions and evidence.

We are proudly technical. We care about the model, the metadata, the policy logic and the integration edge. We also know that none of it matters unless people can understand it, use it and stand behind the outcome.

OUR STORY

Reasoned thinking. Responsible progress.

Datalogos brings reasoned thought and responsible digital progress together. The company was shaped by a practical challenge: organisations need to move forward with data and AI, but policies alone do not guide every decision in the flow of work. AIdentity helps leaders set direction and govern change; Counterpoise turns those boundaries into modular workflows; Verity makes the evidence behind data assurance visible; Datafolio carries selected records, permissions and proof between services; and Hyphvera connects governed data across federated source systems while preserving their ownership boundaries. Customers can begin with the challenge in front of them and expand the capabilities as their needs develop.

LOCAL PROOF OF CONCEPT · VERSION 1.0

FROM CONCEPT TO WORKING EVIDENCE

See governance in action.

The Datalogos local POC shows how Counterpoise, Verity and Datafolio work together in a governed data workflow, from data checks and policy decisions to controlled action and portable evidence.

CounterpoiseEvaluates Governance, Privacy, Ethics and Security rules, routes human review and allows or blocks an action.
VerityInspects data quality, declared provenance and integrity fingerprints, including row-level findings.
DatafolioRecords portable evidence and demonstrates scoped sharing, expiry, verification and revocation.

This is a local demonstration using synthetic or user-supplied CSV data. It runs without cloud accounts or paid services. The aggregation is deterministic and does not call an LLM. It demonstrates workflow behaviour, not production readiness or independent certification.

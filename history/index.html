01 DATA ADVISORY

Build a data foundation
that holds up.

For data leaders reconciling critical data across SaaS, cloud and legacy estates, we define a usable architecture and operating model: which sources are authoritative, who owns them, how quality is measured and how lineage reaches reports, models and decisions.

02 / DATA ASSURANCE✓

Verity

Know what you can trust.

For data owners, risk teams and auditors who need to explain a dataset or AI decision, Verity links source and lineage to validation rules, versions, approvals, changes and evidence. It can show what passed, what is missing and what needs review. Evidence supports assurance; it does not make a source claim true by itself.

ABOUT DATALOGOS

We're here for the
hard questions.

The data and AI conversation is full of promise. It is also full of decisions that cannot be left to chance.

Datalogos Limited is a UK-based challenger built around a conviction: governance should make progress possible. We bring executive advisory and practical technology into the same conversation—connecting business intent to architecture, decisions and evidence.

We are proudly technical. We care about the model, the metadata, the policy logic and the integration edge. We also know that none of it matters unless people can understand it, use it and stand behind the outcome.

OUR STORY

Reasoned thinking. Responsible progress.

Datalogos brings reasoned thought and responsible digital progress together. The company was shaped by a practical challenge: organisations need to move forward with data and AI, but policies alone do not guide every decision in the flow of work. AIdentity helps leaders set direction and govern change; Counterpoise turns those boundaries into modular workflows; Verity makes the evidence behind data assurance visible; and Datafolio carries selected records, permissions and proof as information moves between services. Customers can begin with the challenge in front of them and expand the capabilities as their needs develop.

OUR MISSION

Steward a portfolio that helps people and organisations make purposeful progress through data, AI and technology.

OUR VISION

A future where trusted data, assured AI and responsible technology create progress people can understand and control.
